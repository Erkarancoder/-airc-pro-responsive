Frequently asked

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes — your plan is applied instantly and you can change it whenever you like.

Is my data private?

All interview data is stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB) — nothing is uploaded to a server.

Do unused features disappear on Free?

No — this build keeps every feature usable so you can try the full product; plan tags simply show what a commercial rollout would gate.
